# Prompts for generated reference videos and images (turn 48)

These go to a video generator (Gemini/Veo or similar) or an image generator. Their output is a **reference** for
animations, effects and models; nothing generated goes into the game without the owner's OK and a licence check.

## Rules shared by every animation video (paste them at the end of each prompt)

> Neutral grey articulated artist's mannequin (wooden-style ball joints at neck, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips,
> knees, ankles; no face, no clothes) so the motion is easy to read and retarget. Full body always in frame, locked
> camera (no cuts, no zoom, no camera shake), plain light-grey studio background with a floor grid, even lighting,
> real-time speed, 24-30 fps, one continuous take, 4-6 seconds. Side view unless stated otherwise.

Why the mannequin: the joints are visible, so it is easy to see (and later copy by hand or with a video-to-motion
tool) where every bone goes. Costume and face come from the character model, not from the video.

## 1. Bifrost: entrance and use (main video)

> A rainbow bridge made of 7-9 loose, glowing, flowing light strands (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo,
> violet), no solid floor, gently curving with no sharp corners, starting from a round glowing pad on a city
> street and rising into the sky. The mannequin walks onto the pad; the strands wrap around it like a U-shaped
> channel and lift it about one metre off the pad. It is carried up and along the curved bridge, floating in a
> relaxed standing pose with a slight forward lean, arms a little open for balance, strands streaming past. After a
> few seconds it reaches a glowing ring portal at the end, slows, and steps down onto the ground on the far side.
> Wide three-quarter view that shows the whole path. [shared rules]

## 2. The Bifrost opening from the sky (the 1-second warning mark of Gjallarhorn)

> Night city street seen from a low angle. The clouds part and a vertical column of rainbow light strands falls
> from the sky straight down to one spot on the street, hitting the ground with a soft shock ring of golden light;
> three golden rings pulse outward on the ground, one every third of a second, while the column hums and
> shimmers. The column stays for about one second, then everything inside the ring is pulled up into it and it
> closes from the bottom up. No characters needed, or a few grey mannequins standing in the ring. 3-4 seconds,
> locked camera.

## 3. Fast ascent (pulled up by the column)

> The grey mannequin stands inside a column of rainbow light. Its knees bend slightly, then it is yanked straight
> up very fast: arms pressed down along the body, legs together, toes pointed, body perfectly straight like an
> arrow, a short stretch/smear of rainbow light below it. It leaves the top of the frame in under half a second.
> [shared rules]

## 4. Arrival (only the reverse teleport: comes down the way it went up)

> A column of rainbow light hits the ground. The grey mannequin shoots down inside it, straight like an arrow (arms
> along the body, legs together, toes pointed down), stops just above the floor, drops the last few centimetres and
> lands softly on both feet with a small knee bend, arms opening for balance, then stands up straight. The column
> fades from the top down with a golden ring on the floor. [shared rules]

## 5. Heimdall surfing on the Bifrost (riding pose, the idle while on a bridge)

> The grey mannequin rides a stream of rainbow light strands like a surfer: side stance, feet shoulder-width
> apart, knees bent, front arm forward and slightly up, back arm low and behind, torso turned toward the travel
> direction. It sways a little as the stream curves left and right and carves one turn each way, keeping balance.
> Loopable: the last frame matches the first. Front three-quarter view. [shared rules]

## 6. Axe throw (left mouse button)

> The grey mannequin throws a one-handed axe overhand: weight on the back foot, axe arm raised behind the head,
> step forward, whip the arm over the shoulder and release at eye height, follow through across the body, then a
> second throw with the other hand (alternating hands). The axe flies in an arc, spinning. Two throws, about 0.6 s
> each. [shared rules]

## 7. Gjallarhorn: blowing the horn (1.5 s)

> The grey mannequin plants its feet wide, raises a long curved horn to its mouth with both hands, leans back
> slightly and blows for one and a half seconds while golden sound rings pulse out from the horn's mouth and a
> golden circle glows on the floor around it; it then lowers the horn. Walking forward slowly while blowing is
> allowed. [shared rules]

## 8. Chamado da Bifrost (charge, grab and throw up)

> The grey mannequin A charges forward fast, low and leaning in, grabs mannequin B by the chest with both hands,
> and in one motion swings B upward and away over its shoulder; B flies high in an arc while A stops and stands. At
> the top of B's arc, a band of rainbow strands catches B and carries it away horizontally out of frame. Side view.
> [shared rules]
